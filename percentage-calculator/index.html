Percentage Formula

Use this formula to calculate a percentage of a number:

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: What is 15% of 200?

Step 1: Divide 15 by 100 = 0.15

Step 2: Multiply 0.15 by 200 = 30

Therefore, 15% of 200 is 30.
