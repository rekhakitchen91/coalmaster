How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The word percent means “per hundred”.

1. Find X% of a number

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 250 = (20 ÷ 100) × 250 = 50.

2. Find what percentage one number is of another

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example: 25 is 25% of 100.

3. Calculate percentage increase or decrease

Percentage change = ((New value − Old value) ÷ Old value) × 100

A positive result means an increase. A negative result means a decrease.

How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Multiply the number by 10 and divide the result by 100. For example, 10% of 500 is 50.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.
