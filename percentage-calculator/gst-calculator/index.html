How to Calculate GST

Adding GST

GST Amount = Original Price × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price + GST Amount

Removing GST from an inclusive price

Original Price = Inclusive Price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Original Price

CGST, SGST and IGST

For an eligible intra-state transaction, GST is generally split equally between CGST and SGST. For an inter-state transaction, IGST generally applies. The correct treatment depends on the transaction and applicable tax rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes. CalcMaster's GST calculator is free to use online.

How do I calculate 18% GST on ₹1,000?

18% of ₹1,000 is ₹180. The total price after adding GST is ₹1,180.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Select “Remove GST from an inclusive price”, enter the total price and choose the applicable GST rate.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST generally apply together to intra-state supplies, while IGST generally applies to inter-state supplies, subject to applicable rules.
